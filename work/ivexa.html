IVEXA — a private AI assistant

An AI assistant I designed and built for my own work — and have used every day for a year.

RoleIdea, architecture, development, operation
PlatformsServer, Windows, iPhone
StatusIn daily use · US patent applications filed

The task

Hosted AI assistants forget the context, keep your data on someone else’s servers and can act without asking. I needed an assistant that remembers, keeps data private and never takes a critical step without my approval.

The solution

  • Self-hosted and private by design
  • Long-term memory across conversations and projects
  • A task engine that checks and tests its own results
  • Face ID approval for critical actions
  • Apps for Windows and iPhone

The result

In daily use for a year. US patent applications filed. Technical details are confidential.
